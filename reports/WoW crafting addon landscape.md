# Crafting addons master math, neglect customers

As of late October 2026, WoW crafting addons are crowded at the "crafting math" layer and thin at the "crafting business" layer. On retail (Midnight, patch 12.1.5), **CraftSim** dominates optimization with more than 10M downloads. **Auctionator** is the shared price and shopping-list backend, and **TradeSkillMaster (TSM)** still owns sale-rate-gated restocking. Around them sits a long tail of narrow overlays for crafting orders, knowledge-point (KP) trackers and alt trackers. On Classic (Era, TBC Anniversary, MoP Classic), the standard kit is Skillet-Classic, Auctionator or TSM, Missing TradeSkills List (MTSL) and a leveling guide, plus a fast-growing fringe of guild-sync and trade-chat-scanner addons that rebuild what retail's crafting orders provide natively. The features every addon now ships are reagent cost and profit display, export of missing reagents to Auctionator, a craft queue, crafting-order profitability and per-character snapshots. The clearest demand signals, though, point away from crafting math. The two most-reacted open requests on CraftSim's tracker ask for **customer history and analytics**. Forum complaints focus on scam-ridden public orders, claim abandonment and fragmented crafter discovery. Multi-alt crafters are juggling three or more single-purpose trackers to follow concentration, Artisan's Moxie and KP. Blizzard's API makes nearly all of this buildable in-game, because Midnight's "secret values" lockdown does not touch crafting, order or auction-house functions. The hard limits are elsewhere. Every claim, post, buy and craft must be driven by a real click. Addon-to-addon messaging is small (255 characters), throttled, and blocked in instances. Anything out-of-game, such as push notifications, cross-realm reputation or community price benchmarks, needs a companion app or website.

> **Source caveat (read before relying on any number).** CurseForge, Wago, WoWInterface, Reddit, warcraft.wiki.gg and the Blizzard forums could not be reached from the research sessions behind this report. Every download count, version number, release date and feature description attributed to those sites comes from **search-engine result snippets or summaries**, not from reading the live pages. Figures marked **†** in this report fall into that category and should be re-checked on the live pages before anything depends on them. Facts read directly include the following: GitHub READMEs and issue trackers (CraftSim, Profession Shopping List, Crafting Orders++, BetterProfessions, GatherMate2), and Blizzard's generated API documentation, mirrored in the Gethe/wow-ui-source `live` branch. No Reddit data was retrieved at all. Player-sentiment evidence therefore rests on Blizzard-forum thread titles and summaries, GitHub issues, and the indirect signal of how many addons exist to patch the same gap.

## CraftSim and Auctionator anchor a layered, fragmented ecosystem

The retail landscape has four layers. Price data sits at the bottom: TSM's AuctionDB, Oribos Exchange data shipped inside addon updates, and in-game scans by Auctionator and RECrystallize ([Oribos Exchange](https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/oribos-exchange); [RECrystallize](https://github.com/AcidWeb/RECrystallize)). Crafting math sits above it, and that layer belongs to **CraftSim**. CraftSim covers concentration optimization, including "concentration profit per point". It also offers best-reagent search, a Craft Queue that exports to an Auctionator shopping list, patron work-order optimization, a craft log, and a scan that optimizes every recipe of a profession at once ([CurseForge: CraftSim](https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/craftsim)). Its GitHub tracker confirms the current modules: Craft Lists with **TSM restock expressions**, Recipe Scan, Specialization Info, Customer History, Simulation Mode and profession-tool optimization. The tracker had **162 open issues** in late October 2026 ([GitHub: CraftSim issues](https://github.com/derfloh205/CraftSim/issues)). Execution and bookkeeping form the top layers: TSM's crafting operations and Ledger, and plusmouse's **Journalator**, which logs AH sales, vendor trades and crafting-order income ([TSM: Crafting Operations](https://support.tradeskillmaster.com/en_US/tsm-addon-documentation/1236068-tsm-addon-crafting-operations); [Journalator](https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/journalator)).

Midnight's patron and public order system pulled in a wave of small order overlays, each positioned as lighter than CraftSim:

- **Crafting Orders++** adds Cost / Reward / Profit / Time columns, a "Don't Buy" reagent list and a "is this quality reachable with cheap mats" indicator ([GitHub](https://github.com/foodmuffin/CraftingOrders-)).
- **BetterProfessions** shows commission net of the Consortium cut, checks reagent stock across the reagent and Warband banks, and adds a panel of specialization nodes for the selected recipe ([GitHub](https://github.com/timurchak/BetterProfessions)).
- **Quick Crafting Orders** runs a guided order workflow. It explicitly "does not calculate profit" and does no unattended crafting ([CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/quickcraftingorders)).

These overlays have 0–1 GitHub stars. They are recent hobby-scale projects, not established tools.

Planning and discovery tools fill out the rest of the map:

- **Profession Shopping List (PSL)** is the closest thing to an all-in-one progression hub. It covers recipe tracking, cooldowns, KP and perks, order profitability, "Quick Order" presets that send work orders to alts, and profession achievements ([GitHub: PSL](https://github.com/Slackluster/ProfessionShoppingList)).
- **CraftScan** scans trade chat for requests you can fill, with pop-ups and respond/dismiss keybinds ([CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/craftscan)).
- **RECraft** and **Lantern** scan the order board and send order notifications ([RECraft](https://www.wowinterface.com/downloads/info26512-RECraft-CraftingOrdersScanner.html); [Lantern](https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/lantern-craftingorders)).
- **TestFlight** and **Enhanced Crafting** are "unlock all reagent slots" simulators. Enhanced Crafting calls its own quality preview a "best-effort estimate" ([TestFlight](https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/testflight); [Enhanced Crafting](https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/enhancedcrafting)).
- **Skillet** has returned to retail as a replacement profession window with a multi-item queue and automatic purchase of vendor reagents ([CurseForge: Skillet](https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/skillet)).
- Progression trackers form their own Midnight-era genre: **WeeklyKnowledge** (an alt-wide KP grid), **Routine**, **Myu's KP Tracker**, **Profession Knowledge Treasures** (auto-routing to uncollected treasures), and the HandyNotes: Midnight map pins ([WeeklyKnowledge](https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/weeklyknowledge); [Profession Knowledge Treasures](https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/profession-knowledge-treasures); [HandyNotes: Midnight](https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/handynotes-midnight-treasures)).

Classic splits into the same families but shifts the weight. Classic Era and TBC Anniversary lack retail's native crafting orders and modern profession API ([LibProfessions](https://github.com/datagutten/wow-LibProfessions) notes "WoW classic has no profession API"). Classic players therefore have many addons that rebuild work orders and crafter discovery:

- addon-built order boards: **Jays Guild Work Orders** (guild-scoped) and **Crafting & Gathering Order – Classic** (realm-wide) ([Jays](https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/jays-guild-work-orders); [Crafting & Gathering Order](https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/crafting-and-gathering-order-classic))
- peer-to-peer guild recipe directories: **TOG Profession Master** and Esperanza's **Profession Master** ([TOGPM](https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/togprofessionmaster); [Profession Master](https://wowinterface.com/downloads/info26220-ProfessionMaster-GuildampPersonal....html))
- trade-chat scanners: **Craft Catcher Suite**, which matches requests against all your alts' recipes ([Craft Catcher Suite](https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/craft-catcher-suite))
- missing-recipe and source databases: **MTSL**, which now survives through a third-party patch for Era and TBC Anniversary ([MTSL patch](https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/missing-tradeskills-list-tbc-anniversary-patch))

Maintenance is the defining Classic problem. Many 2026 addons ship separate per-client builds, and a 7 July 2026 TBC Anniversary UI update broke "many Classic addons" ([Warcraft Tavern](https://www.warcrafttavern.com/tbc/news/new-nameplates-are-live-in-tbc-anniversary-many-addons-are-broken/)).

## Five features every serious crafting addon now ships

These features appear in enough addons that a new addon without them will look incomplete next to existing tools.

| # | Table-stakes feature | Where it already appears | Note for a new addon |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Reagent cost and profit in the profession window**, using a pluggable price source (Auctionator, TSM, Oribos Exchange, RECrystallize) | Auctionator, CraftSim, TestFlight, Crafting Orders++, BetterProfessions, TSM, ProfitSort and others | CraftSim supports four sources, and a PR proposes a fifth ([PR #1525](https://github.com/derfloh205/CraftSim/pull/1525)). Users expect "works with what I already run". |
| 2 | **Export of missing reagents to an Auctionator shopping list**, net of what you already own | CraftSim, Crafting Orders++, QCO, Craft Shopper, Shopper, Requisition, CraftRoute, ProfitSort | Auctionator lists are the de facto interchange format. Craft Shopper subtracts bags, bank and Warband bank stock ([Craft Shopper](https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/craft-shopper)). |
| 3 | **Craft queue with one click per craft** | CraftSim, TSM, Skillet, PSL, Vanthe QoL, CraftSimConcQueue | No addon offers unattended crafting. QCO explicitly disclaims it ([QCO](https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/quickcraftingorders)). |
| 4 | **Crafting-order profitability** (reward value minus reagent cost), retail only | CraftSim, PSL, Crafting Orders++, BetterProfessions, TestFlight | Retail differentiators now include net-of-Consortium-cut, reagent-provider awareness and minimum-quality checks. |
| 5 | **Per-character snapshots viewable across alts** | WeeklyKnowledge, Routine, Altoholic, Shopping Forever, Skillet-Classic, Profession Master | Retail progression trackers also treat KP checklists with waypoints as baseline ([Routine](https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/midnight-routine); [Midnight Profession Tracker](https://www.wowinterface.com/downloads/info27112-MidnightProfessionTracker.html)). |

Retail adds **concentration awareness** to the baseline. It ranges from CraftSim's per-point optimization to QCO's simple settings and BetterProfessions' "concentration needed for minimum quality" ([BetterProfessions](https://github.com/timurchak/BetterProfessions)). Classic's baseline is older and wider. It covers a larger searchable window, a cross-alt shopping list, missing-recipe lists with sources, 1–300 leveling routes with mats lists, "can my alt learn this" tooltips, guild crafter lookup and cooldown tracking. Every one of these appears in at least two maintained Classic addons ([Skillet-Classic](https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/skillet-classic); [CanMyAltUseThis](https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/canmyaltusethis); [WhatsCrafting](https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/whats-crafting)).

The rare and unique features show where existing authors have found differentiation:

- **CraftSim:** a permutation-based finishing-reagent optimizer ([23.3.0](https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/craftsim/files/7854843)), concentration profit per point, TSM restock expressions inside Craft Lists, Customer History, and recipe export to the crafting-order marketplaces Easycraft and ForgeFinder ([CurseForge: CraftSim](https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/craftsim)).
- **TSM:** sale-rate-gated restock through custom strings such as `ifgt(DBRegionSaleRate, 0.3, 50g)` ([girlgoblin](https://girlgoblin.ca/2022/02/27/deep-dive-restocking-with-tsm/)). The sale-rate data behind it is crowd-sourced through the TSM App, because Blizzard's API has no sales data ([Kamoo's TSM App](https://github.com/kamoo1/Kamoo-s-TSM-App)). That makes sale-rate the hardest feature for a newcomer to replicate.
- **PSL:** alt work-order presets, profession-achievement recipe fetching, and portable anvil and cooking fire buttons.
- **Profession Knowledge Treasures:** an auto-advancing treasure route.
- **Enhanced Crafting:** guild crafter search with predicted quality given your materials.
- **Skillet:** automatic purchase of vendor reagents for the queue.
- **Classic:** TOGPM's hash-then-fetch peer-to-peer guild sync, and Craft Catcher Suite's matching of trade-chat requests against every alt's recipes.

Each of these is a single-addon feature, which leaves room to combine several of them in one product.

TSM is rated lowest on ease of use ([tier list](https://www.wowforever.games/563.html)). Wowhead publishes a separate spec guide for every profession ([Wowhead](https://www.wowhead.com/guides/professions)) | Zygor (paid) for leveling; no spec advisor addon found | **Medium (mostly inferred)** |
| 10 | **Retail missing-recipe + source finder** | ATT is called overkill for the job ([forums](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/t/addon-for-misisng-profession-recipes/1325466)). Missing Recipes needs each alt to run `/miss` ([CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/missing-recipes)) | Weak on retail; MTSL is Classic-only | **Low-medium** |
| 11 | **One addon across Classic clients** (Era, TBC Anniversary, MoP) | Per-client forks everywhere; the 7 Jul 2026 breakage ([Warcraft Tavern](https://www.warcrafttavern.com/tbc/news/new-nameplates-are-live-in-tbc-anniversary-many-addons-are-broken/)) | TOGPM covers all Classic clients; most others do not | **Low-medium (inferred)** |
| 12 | **Out-of-game notifications** (order arrives while away) | Indirect: RECraft's semi-AFK scanning; claim-timeout pain | **None found**; in-game pop-ups only | **Low-medium** |
| 13 | Disenchant / salvage helpers; cross-realm discovery; forward planning for upcoming patches | One forum thread for disenchanting ([forums](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/t/is-there-really-no-updated-addon-for-disenchanting/1782733)); no direct evidence for the other two | Sparse | **Low** |

Two features of this ranking matter for product strategy. First, the top demand is **business tooling**: history, filtering, scheduling and discovery. It is not a better simulator. CraftSim already owns simulation, and its open bugs ("Recipe Info average profit per craft is significantly off" #1527, Midnight recipe Lua errors #1505, "Login bug since 12.1.5" #1538) show how costly it is to keep Blizzard's crafting math correct every patch ([CraftSim issues](https://github.com/derfloh205/CraftSim/issues)). Second, Blizzard's own changes in TWW and Midnight focused on progression currency and NPC demand (patron orders, flicker orders, Moxie, a one-time KP reset). They have not addressed player-to-player marketplace infrastructure, so this whitespace is unlikely to be closed by a Blizzard patch soon ([Wowhead](https://www.wowhead.com/news/patron-work-order-changes-higher-reagent-chance-removed-work-orders-346872); [Wowhead: Moxie](https://www.wowhead.com/guide/midnight/professions/knowledge-points-artisans-moxie)). The counter-argument is that the forum population is split between "too complex" and "don't simplify crafting" ([forums](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/t/going-into-midnight-i-hope-they-dont-simplify-crafting-too-much/2164422)). An onboarding-first product therefore competes for a less committed audience than a power-crafter CRM does.

## The API permits almost everything except unattended action and off-client reach

Blizzard's generated API docs for the live Midnight client show a rich, mostly unrestricted read surface ([API docs](https://github.com/Gethe/wow-ui-source/blob/live/Interface/AddOns/Blizzard_APIDocumentationGenerated/)):

- `C_TradeSkillUI` (88 functions) exposes schematics, skill against difficulty, quality thresholds, `concentrationCost` and `ingenuityRefund`.
- `C_CraftingOrders` (36 functions) lists, claims, fulfills and places orders.
- `C_ProfSpecs` (27 functions) reads specialization trees.
- `C_Item.GetItemCount` can include the Warband bank.

Only ten crafting, auction-house and bank functions carry the `HasRestrictions` flag: `ClaimOrder`, `PlaceNewOrder`, `PostItem`, `PostCommodity`, `ConfirmPostItem`, `ConfirmPostCommodity`, `StartCommoditiesPurchase`, `PlaceBid`, `CancelAuction` and `PurchaseBankTab`. In practice these need a real click ([CraftingOrderUI docs](https://github.com/Gethe/wow-ui-source/blob/live/Interface/AddOns/Blizzard_APIDocumentationGenerated/CraftingOrderUIDocumentation.lua); [AuctionHouse docs](https://github.com/Gethe/wow-ui-source/blob/live/Interface/AddOns/Blizzard_APIDocumentationGenerated/AuctionHouseDocumentation.lua)). `CraftRecipe` is *not* flagged. Even so, CraftSim crafts one item per click behind a click-lock ([CraftQueue UI](https://github.com/derfloh205/CraftSim/blob/main/Modules/CraftQueue/UI.lua)), and the only safe design assumption is click-driven crafting. Opening the Professions frame itself reportedly needs a hardware event (snippet of [ProfessionDataInitializer](https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/professiondatainitializer)†). The doc files do not say whether `HasRestrictions` means hardware-event-only or fully protected, and the wiki pages that would settle it were unreachable.

Midnight's 12.0 lockdown adds the following ([RestrictedActions docs](https://github.com/Gethe/wow-ui-source/blob/live/Interface/AddOns/Blizzard_APIDocumentationGenerated/RestrictedActionsDocumentation.lua); [ChatInfo docs](https://github.com/Gethe/wow-ui-source/blob/live/Interface/AddOns/Blizzard_APIDocumentationGenerated/ChatInfoDocumentation.lua)):

- **secret values**
- restriction states: Combat, Encounter, ChallengeMode, PvPMatch, Map and Chat
- removal of `COMBAT_LOG_EVENT_UNFILTERED`
- a block on addon communications in instances

Parsing the docs found **no secret-value flags on any TradeSkill, CraftingOrder, ProfSpecs, AuctionHouse, Bank or Item function**. Secret-value flags appear only in ChatInfo and GuildInfo. While in an instance, chat reaches Lua as secret values and addons cannot send comms (snippet of [Patch 12.0.0 API changes](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Patch_12.0.0/API_changes)†). `SendAddonMessage` now returns `AddOnMessageLockdown` and `TargetOffline` result codes. Addon messages are capped at **255 characters** with a 16-character prefix. Each prefix allows a burst of **10 messages, regenerating one per second**, and the server can change these defaults (snippet of [warcraft.wiki.gg](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/API_C_ChatInfo.SendAddonMessage)†; 255 cap also at [wowprogramming](https://wowprogramming.com/docs/api/SendAddonMessage.html)). Addons not flagged for 12.0+ do not load by default ([Blizzard Watch](https://blizzardwatch.com/2026/01/13/addon-apocalypse-midnight/)†). Classic builds have the secret-value system "entirely disabled" (snippet of [Patch 12.0.5 API changes](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Patch_12.0.5/API_changes)†).

|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Persist to account-wide SavedVariables. Avoid CraftSim's #1540 trap (pricing a craft with the "last hooked" recipe) by keying on order IDs |
| 2. Order filtering / valuation | **Feasible; claiming stays manual** | `RequestCrafterOrders`/`GetCrafterOrders` readable; `ClaimOrder` is restricted; order data available only at a crafting-orders table after `CRAFTINGORDERS_CAN_REQUEST` | Rank, hide and flag orders automatically, then offer "claim this" as one click per claim. Auto-sniping or timed claiming is out |
| 3. Multi-alt dashboard + concentration scheduling | **Feasible with staleness** | No `C_TradeSkillUI` function takes a character argument; Warband bank counts via `GetItemCount(..., includeAccountBank)`; concentration ID per skill line. Regen reported as ~10/hour, 1,000 cap ([Icy Veins](https://icy-veins.com/wow/professions)†) | Snapshot on login or profession open, extrapolate regenerating resources offline, and show "last seen" ages. Scheduling is pure client-side math |
| 4. Cross-channel discovery | **Feasible in-game, bounded** | Open-world chat parsing works; in instances chat arrives as secret values and comms are blocked (`IsAddOnRestrictionActive(Chat)`, `ADDON_RESTRICTION_STATE_CHANGED`) | Queue alerts and messages until the restriction lifts. Coverage is limited to channels the player sees. Peer features hit the install-base problem |

| 6. Reputation | **Not feasible in-game alone** | Addon comms: 255 chars, 10-burst/1 per second, guild/group/whisper scope, blocked in instances | A trustworthy, cross-realm reputation needs an external service plus a companion app reading SavedVariables, which are written only on logout or `/reload`. In-game peer-to-peer reputation is trivially gameable |
| 7. Guild "who can craft at what quality" | **Feasible (two tiers)** | `C_GuildInfo.QueryGuildMembersForRecipe` / `QueryGuildMemberRecipes` need no peer addon but expose no quality or spec data | Use the native query for "who knows it". | 8. Commission guidance | **Personal: feasible. Community: needs external pipeline** | `CalculateCraftingOrderPostingFee`; own order history from gap 1 | Personal "what you've been paid" benchmarks fall out of the CRM. Realm-wide benchmarks need TSM-App-style uploads |

| 10. Retail missing recipes + sources | **Feasible; data-heavy** | `TradeSkillRecipeInfo.learned`, `GetRecipeDescription`; Crafting Orders++ shows Blizzard source text for unlearned recipes | The work is curating source data each patch. | 11. Cross-Classic-client support | **Feasible; costly** | Classic uses an older, narrower tradeskill API, plus Era's separate Craft window; no secret values | Separate code paths per flavor. Budget for client UI updates like 7 Jul 2026 |
| 12. Out-of-game notifications | **Not feasible as real-time push** | Addons have no network access; SavedVariables flush only on logout or `/reload` | At most an after-session digest through a companion app. Do not market "mobile alerts" |
| — Unattended crafting, auto-claim, auto-post loops | **Not feasible** (anti-feature) | Restricted functions need a hardware event each; Midnight tightened automation generally | Advertise one-click-per-step honestly, as QCO does |

Two cross-cutting risks follow from this. Maintenance is the bigger one. Every 12.x minor patch has produced taint and data regressions in order flows (CraftSim #1342 MoneyFrame taint, #1502 ADDON_ACTION_FORBIDDEN; PSL's patron-currency break fixed in 12.0.1-05) ([CraftSim issues](https://github.com/derfloh205/CraftSim/issues); [PSL file](https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/profession-shopping-list/files/7708996)†). Features that sit on Blizzard's order UI inherit that churn. The second risk is unresolved game-design facts that affect data models. Sources conflict on whether Midnight crafted gear kept 5 quality ranks or dropped to 3, and on whether the KP respec arrived in 12.0.5 or 12.1 ([Wowhead](https://www.wowhead.com/news/simplifying-professions-in-midnight-lowest-two-ranks-of-crafted-quality-removed-378355); [Overgear](https://overgear.com/guides/wow/midnight-profession-changes/)). Read quality tiers from `GetQualitiesForRecipe` at runtime and never hard-code them.

## Conclusion

The crowded part of this market is the engine room. A new addon that leads with another simulator or another Auctionator shopping-list exporter enters a field where CraftSim has 10M+ downloads† and half a dozen free overlays copy the basics. The defensible opening is the crafter's *business ledger*: customer history, order triage and filtering, multi-alt concentration scheduling, and personal commission benchmarks built from that history. It answers the most-reacted requests on the market leader's tracker. Every part of it runs on unrestricted read APIs and SavedVariables, and it can consume CraftSim's published events instead of re-deriving Blizzard's crafting math each patch. Discovery and reputation are the natural second act, but the API sets a hard boundary there: in-game messaging is too small, too throttled and too gameable to carry trust. Anything that needs to be realm-wide, cross-realm or out-of-game needs a companion app, and that is a different and much larger product decision.

Before committing, close the evidence gaps that this research could not. Pull Reddit and CurseForge comment threads to test whether casual crafters share the power users' CRM demand. Verify download counts and last-update dates on the live CurseForge and Wago pages. Read the warcraft.wiki.gg pages for `CraftRecipe` and the 12.1 API changes to confirm the exact restriction semantics. Then check that the forum-thread signal on order-board quality, much of it captured only as search summaries, holds up in the full threads.
